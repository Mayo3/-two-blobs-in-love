# Privacy policy: Two Blobs Uploader

Two Blobs Uploader is the posting script of the "Two Blobs in Love" channel (this repository). It is used only by
the channel's owner, to upload the channel's own daily video to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and
Instagram account.

- **What it accesses:** permission to upload videos to YouTube (`youtube.upload`), and permission to publish
  Reels to the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram account. It does not read, store or share anyone's
  personal data, messages, contacts or viewing history.
- **Where credentials are kept:** the login tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets of this
  repository and are used only to upload the day's video.
- **Third parties:** nothing is sold or shared. Videos go only to the platforms named above.
- **Removing access:** revoke it any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook's
  *Settings → Business integrations*.

Contact: open an issue in this repository.
